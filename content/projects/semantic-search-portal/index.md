## Context

Autonomous driving teams need specific, often rare, driving scenarios from fleet data to retrain and validate their models. Finding them used to take days.

## My role

As Technical Product Owner, I rolled out a semantic search portal for fleet driving data.

## Outcome

- Scenario discovery time cut **from days to minutes**.
- Teams can curate **safety-relevant and long-tail cases** for model retraining and validation.
